Firefox PDF Reader

Distraction-free PDF reading. Double-click a PDF and you get its pages in a window of their own, black with white text, scrolling smoothly, with pictures in their own colours, and nothing else.

Try it

A browser version of the reader showing an example paper. Scroll it, press F or double-click for full screen, or open it on its own page. It remembers where you were.

Features

  • Black pages, white textEvery page is black and its text white; pictures (photographs, scans, X-ray and CT images) and the colours of charts stay exactly as the PDF has them, and text on a picture or a coloured box stays readable.
  • One window per PDFEach PDF opens in a window of its own, named after its file; Alt+Tab (Cmd+` on a Mac) switches between them.
  • Full screen, with a way outWindows open filling the screen with no tabs, address bar or menus. F11 makes one full screen and F11 again leaves (Ctrl+Cmd+F and Esc on a Mac).
  • Nothing but the PDFNo toolbar, sidebar or notice bars.
  • ContinuousOne long vertical scroll through every page, fit to the page width.
  • Remembers your spotReopen a PDF where you left off, at the same zoom.
  • Smooth scrollingFirefox's smooth scrolling, tuned to work with your mouse's.
  • Low latencyPages drawn on the graphics card (WebRender), and no add-ons or start page in the way.
  • Separate from your browserIts own Firefox profile; your normal Firefox is untouched. To show PDFs in its own viewer, the reader lets a page opened from your disk read your other files, in that profile only, so don't open or drop HTML files you don't trust onto its windows. Web pages can't read your files.
  • Default PDF appDouble-click any PDF to open it here.
  • Every desktopWindows, macOS, Linux and ChromeOS, tested on each.
